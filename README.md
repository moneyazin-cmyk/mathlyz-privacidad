math lyz Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 12, 2026

math lyz is a calculator that solves math problems step by step. This policy explains which data is used and why.

Data the app stores on your phone
Your name (the one you type at the start), your chosen language, your calculation history and your solalas balance.
This data is stored only on your device. It is never sent to any server and we cannot see it.
Calculations run on the phone itself, without an internet connection.
You can delete it at any time from Android Settings → Apps → math lyz → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.
Ads (Google AdMob)
The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads: a banner at the bottom and optional rewarded ads that give solalas. To serve ads, Google may collect and process:

the device's advertising ID;
the IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
information about the device and interactions with the ads;
diagnostic and crash data from the ads SDK.
Google processes this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent
In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's official platform before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app. You can also reset or turn off your advertising ID in Android settings.

Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.
